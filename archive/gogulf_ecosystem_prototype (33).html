Official Government Update

UAE Announces Expanded Golden Visa Categories for Tech Professionals

Source: ICP Official Press Release

Published Oct 24, 2026 • 3 min read

The Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security (ICP) has announced a significant expansion to the Golden Visa program, specifically targeting software engineers, AI specialists, and data scientists.

In a move designed to cement the UAE's position as a global technological hub, the new directives lower the salary threshold for highly specialized tech roles and simplify the educational credential validation process.

Key Changes to the Eligibility Criteria

  • Salary Threshold: The minimum monthly salary requirement for categories including AI Engineers, Blockchain Developers, and Data Scientists has been adjusted to AED 25,000 (down from AED 30,000).
  • Educational Equivalency: Fast-track equivalency processing for degrees from Top 500 global universities.
  • Startups: Founders of tech startups recognized by the Ministry of Economy now have a streamlined application process.

"This expansion is a testament to our commitment to building a knowledge-based economy. We are opening our doors wider to the world's brightest minds." — Official Spokesperson, ICP.

Applicants can begin submitting their requests through the official ICP portal or via authorized typing centers starting next week. Existing residents in these professions are encouraged to apply for status adjustment.
