Service agreement · Key terms

Review the terms below before entering the agreement stage. The final generated agreement should be reviewed against the full approved legal text.

1. Services

Career counselling and advisory, vacancy information, working/living-condition information and other services agreed between the parties.

2. Confidentiality

Candidate information should be handled confidentially and used only for relevant proposal, advisory and process purposes, subject to applicable law and authorized disclosures.

3. Provider role and disclaimer

The source document describes the company as a career counsellor/advisor, not an MEA-authorized recruiting agency. Any recruitment, employment offer, permit, visa or travel arrangement is subject to the responsible employer, authorized agency and relevant authorities.

4. Processing, refund and cancellation

Timelines depend on employer, documentation, embassy/authority availability and case circumstances. Refund and cancellation conditions must be read in the complete agreement and clarified in writing before payment. Do not rely on this summary as a replacement for the complete clauses.

5. Candidate declaration

The candidate confirms that information and documents supplied are accurate, that the service scope and commercial terms have been reviewed, and that they voluntarily consent to the agreement.
